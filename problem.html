What These Context Metrics Mean

Poverty (`pov20`)

Meaning: Share of residents in poverty in 2020.

Scale: 0 to 1 (shown as %).

Interpretation: Higher values usually indicate more economic constraint in a neighborhood.

Upward Mobility (`mob_up`)

Meaning: Long-run mobility indicator (chance of moving up economically).

Scale: 0 to 1 (higher is better).

Interpretation: Higher values suggest stronger local mobility outcomes.

Income (`inc20`)

Meaning: Median household income in 2020.

Scale: USD.

Interpretation: Higher values indicate stronger local household earnings context.

Opportunity Gap (`opp_gap`)

Meaning: Neighborhood opportunity disparity measure.

Scale: Numeric index.

Interpretation: Larger magnitude reflects wider distance between current and potential outcomes.

Disadvantage Flag (`flag_disadv`)

Meaning: Binary indicator for disadvantaged tracts.

Scale: 0 = No, 1 = Yes.

Interpretation: Used for context and support targeting, not for limiting options.

Youth Group (`youth_group`)

Meaning: Cluster label from Round 2 segmentation.

Values: Very Disadvantaged, Disadvantaged, Moderate Opportunity, Higher Opportunity.

Interpretation: Starting context category used to personalize guidance.
