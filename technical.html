Computation Reference

  • Risk score: sum of 4 interpretable binary rules from task composition and job zone.
  • KNN imputation: missing mobility-related fields estimated from 5 nearest tracts.
  • Segmentation: KMeans on standardized pov20, mob_up, inc20, opp_gap.
  • Career match: weighted blend of normalized safety and accessibility.
  • Education ROI: group means from tract-level Scorecard/OES fields.
All displayed metrics are derived from uploaded CSV/JSON exports in data/.
