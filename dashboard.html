Education and Labor Metric Definitions

Education Cost (`sc_cost_mean`)

Meaning: Average total education cost context from Scorecard-derived fields.

Scale: USD.

Use: Higher values increase affordability pressure in pathway decisions.

Earnings 6/10 Years (`sc_earn6_mean`, `sc_earn10_mean`)

Meaning: Average earnings outcome context after entry.

Scale: USD.

Use: Used to compare long-term return potential across youth groups.

Employment Change (`oes_emp_chg`)

Meaning: Change in employment level from OES context variables.

Scale: Numeric change.

Use: Indicates whether labor demand context is growing or shrinking.

Average Wage Change (`oes_aw_chg`)

Meaning: Average wage change context from OES.

Scale: Numeric change (USD context).

Use: Adds labor-market trend context to ROI interpretation.
